The first build under the MacPUMP name, shared privately before the public 1.2.1 release. Compared with 1.1.0:

- Renamed to MacPUMP (Mac Power Usage Monitoring Program), with **Lite and Advanced** modes and a choice between them on first run.
- A **dashboard window** with a sidebar: Power, Power History, CPU, GPU, Memory, Disk, Network, Activity, Apps, Temperature and Sensors tabs, each with local history where it applies.
- A **power distribution diagram** (adapter, battery and Mac) in the dashboard and the popover.
- A **customisable menu bar popover** (layouts and blocks), an optional floating overlay and menu bar text templates.
- **Alerts** on many readings, charge reminders (unplug / plug in), quiet hours and an alert log.
- **Settings** in a window with a sidebar: profiles, import and export of settings, keyboard shortcuts, `macpump://` links and a `--status` command.
- **CSV export** for every history from the File menu, plus “Export Everything (Last 7 Days)”.
- A battery health trend, a weekly report, a storage panel and a diagnostics pane that shows MacPUMP’s own cost.
- Show MacPUMP in the menu bar, the Dock, or both.
- Energy work: less redrawing, fewer wakeups and adaptive refresh.
- A charging-limit experiment was built and then removed, because the Mac it was developed on does not expose a charging control. MacPUMP does not change how your Mac charges.

**Signing:** ad-hoc signed with the hardened runtime; not signed with a Developer ID and not notarized.
