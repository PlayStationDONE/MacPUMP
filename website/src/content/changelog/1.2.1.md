The first official, public release of MacPUMP. Free to download and use.

- Live power distribution between charger, battery and Mac, with battery health, cycles and runtime.
- Which apps drain your battery, and what keeps your Mac awake.
- CPU, GPU, memory, disk, network, temperature and sensor pages (Advanced mode).
- History up to 30 days, energy used, a battery health trend, a weekly report and CSV export.
- A customisable menu bar popover, a floating power meter, alerts and charge reminders.
- Lite and Advanced modes; menu bar, Dock or both; Light and Dark mode.
- No account, no tracking and no network connections.

**Download:** `MacPUMP-1.2.1.dmg` (recommended) or `MacPUMP-1.2.1.zip`, for Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later.

**Signing:** ad-hoc signed with the hardened runtime; not signed with a Developer ID and not notarized. On first launch, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
