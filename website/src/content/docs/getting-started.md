## What you need

- **macOS 14 Sonoma or later.**
- **A Mac with Apple silicon** (M1 or later) for the downloadable build. MacPUMP is built for MacBooks with a battery.
- No administrator password. MacPUMP runs with your normal user rights.

## Install

1. Download the MacPUMP ZIP and double-click it.
2. Drag **MacPUMP.app** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it.

### The first time you open it

This build is **not notarized** by Apple yet, so macOS says it can’t check the app. That’s expected. To open it:

1. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to MacPUMP.
3. Confirm. You only need to do this once.

## Lite or Advanced

On first run MacPUMP asks how much you want to see.

- **Lite** shows the essentials: Power, Power History, Apps and Temperature, and a short Settings page.
- **Advanced** shows everything: CPU, GPU, Memory, Disk, Network, Activity and Sensors tabs, alerts, profiles, the overlay and every setting.

You can switch at any time in Settings. Nothing is deleted when you switch, and anything you turned on in Advanced keeps working in Lite.

## Finding your way around

- **Click the MacPUMP icon** in the menu bar to open the popover: the battery bar, the power-flow diagram, time left and power by app.
- **Press ⌥⌘P** to open the popover from anywhere, even when the menu bar is too full to show the icon.
- **Right-click the icon** for quick options, *Open Dashboard…*, *Open at Login* and *Quit*.
- **Open the dashboard** from that menu, or with the chart button in the popover, for every reading and its history.
- **Settings** (⌘, from the menu) holds launch at login, Dock visibility, appearance, history and privacy options.

You can show MacPUMP in the menu bar, the Dock or both: **Settings › General › Show MacPUMP in**.

## Permissions

MacPUMP needs no special permission to monitor your Mac. macOS asks once whether MacPUMP may show notifications, the first time you turn on a reminder or alert. *Open at Login* uses the standard macOS login item setting.
