## Charge reminders

Set a **target** and a **floor** with the charge markers on the battery bar (see [Reading the power diagram](/docs/power-flow)), or turn on the unplug and plug-in reminders. MacPUMP notifies you when your charge crosses them.

Reminders only notify. **MacPUMP never limits or changes how your Mac charges.**

## Alerts (Advanced)

Create alerts on many readings, including battery, temperature, CPU, memory, disk space and network. Each alert fires when a reading goes above or below a level you choose.

- **Quiet hours** silence notifications at times you pick.
- **An alert log** shows what fired and what was done.
- **A Shortcut** can run when an alert fires, if you choose one.

In Lite mode, two simple switches cover the common cases: a low battery alert and a hot Mac alert.

## Notifications permission

macOS asks once whether MacPUMP may show notifications, the first time you turn on a reminder or alert. If you never use them, you’re never asked.
