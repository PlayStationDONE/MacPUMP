If MacPUMP is running but you can’t see its icon, your menu bar is almost certainly **full**. It hasn’t failed.

macOS places menu bar icons from the right edge leftward. When that row is full, the next icon is pushed past the notch into the area where the frontmost app shows its menus, and those menus cover it. MacPUMP is still running; you just can’t see it.

## What to do

- **Press ⌥⌘P.** The popover opens at the top right of the screen whether or not the icon is visible.
- **Free some space.** Quit another menu bar app, or set a couple of Control Center items (Bluetooth, Sound, Screen Mirroring, Stage Manager, Now Playing) to *Don’t Show in Menu Bar* in **System Settings › Control Center**. MacPUMP appears straight away.
- **Reorder icons** by holding ⌘ and dragging them, once MacPUMP is visible.
- **Use a menu bar manager**, such as Ice, Bartender or Hidden Bar, to fix it for good.
- **Show MacPUMP in the Dock** instead: **Settings › General › Show MacPUMP in**.

## Menu bar styles

The icon is shown on its own by default, because the narrowest item is the one most likely to fit. From the right-click menu you can add the charge percentage or the wattage beside it.

In Advanced mode you can also write your own menu bar text. See [Shortcuts, links and scripts](/docs/automation) for the available values.
